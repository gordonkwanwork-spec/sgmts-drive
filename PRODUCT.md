# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People playing a browser-based driving game on desktop or touch devices. They choose a service, drive an articulated electric vehicle along the Hung Shui Kiu / Ha Tsuen corridor, stop at stations, and may explore the route or operate the control centre.

## Product Purpose

Make a seven-station transit service playable and understandable. The interface should help players choose a journey, read the next stop and vehicle state at a glance, and act quickly while the 3D corridor remains visible.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clean, readable. The requested material cue is glass over water: transparent enough to connect the controls to the game world, restrained enough to keep labels and states clear.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboard cards, opaque blocks that hide the corridor, excessive borders and glow, strong blur, and decorative motion that competes with driving. The game's approved SGMTS Drive logo and its blue, cyan, and orange roles remain the identity anchor.

## Design Principles

- Show the journey and the game world first; keep controls legible without covering the scene unnecessarily.
- Give one clear primary action at each step, with secondary controls present but quieter.
- Use translucent surfaces only where they improve orientation or depth, and keep their text contrast dependable.
- Preserve the same interaction vocabulary across light and dark themes, driving controls, and the control centre.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text and controls highly readable across varied game scenery and both themes. Maintain keyboard focus, touch targets, visible selected states, and reduced-motion support. Use subtle blur and motion during gameplay.
